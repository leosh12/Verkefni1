Lobotomy Corporation

Lobotomy Corporation is a game where you, the manager of Lobotomy Corporation, must manage a facility of "Abnormalities".
over time you, the player, will start to learn morea and more about lobotomy Corporation, it's Abnormalities, what Abnormalities are and what is going on.

>

What does Lobotomy Corporation do?

Lobotomy Corporation, or L-corp, is a company that specializes in the containment and study of Abnormalities.

Lobotomy Corporation harvests energy from the Abnormalities to power "The City". Yet they also use abnromalities to create E.G.O weapons and suits.
